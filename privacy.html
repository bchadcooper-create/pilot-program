Privacy Policy

Flight Crew Fitness · Last updated 11 August 2026

Flight Crew Fitness ("the app") is a training and nutrition tool for airline crew. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and what control you have over it.

What is stored

  • Account: your email address, used to sign in and to sync your data across devices.
  • Training data: workouts, sets, reps, weights, durations and personal records you enter.
  • Health and body data: body weight, waist measurement, blood pressure and fasting glucose, if you choose to record them. Sleep, readiness and activity data if you connect an Oura Ring.
  • Nutrition data: meals and food items you log, including their nutritional values.
  • Flight schedule: if you upload a crew schedule file, its contents and the original file are stored so the app can plan around your duty periods.
  • Photos of food: sent for analysis and used to produce a nutrition estimate. See below.

Food photos

When you photograph a meal, the image is sent to Anthropic's API to identify the food and estimate its nutrition. The image is transmitted for that purpose only. It is not stored on our servers after analysis, and it is not used to train any model. The resulting text description and nutrition figures are saved with your meal log; the photograph itself is not.

Apple Health

If you grant access, the app reads and writes workout, body measurement and sleep data through Apple HealthKit. Health data obtained through HealthKit is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for any purpose other than showing you your own information inside the app.

What is never done

  • Your data is never sold.
  • Your data is never shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • There is no advertising in the app and no advertising identifier is collected.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs are embedded.

Leaderboards

If you set a call sign, that call sign and the associated lift figures appear on public leaderboards inside the app. Leave the call sign blank to stay off them entirely; your lifts remain private either way until you set one.

Service providers

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication). Food photo analysis uses Anthropic. Oura data is retrieved from Oura Health only with your explicit authorisation, which you can revoke at any time from the Devices screen.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and everything stored with it from within the app: Menu → Profile → Delete Account. This removes your workouts, meals, biometrics, schedule and account record. It is immediate and cannot be undone. You can export your data to CSV beforehand from Menu → Data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be noted by the date above and surfaced in the app.
